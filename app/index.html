1. Pick a sign

rest is your relaxed hand and random movement between signs. Record it as often as any sign: it's what keeps the glove quiet when you're not signing.

2. Record it

Press Record (or the glove button). After the 3-second countdown, hold or perform the sign for 1.2 seconds. Aim for 30+ per sign, from every teammate.

Guided dataset session

Records the ticked signs in turns, least-recorded first, until each one reaches the target. Hands-free: follow the big prompt and do each sign after the countdown.

3. Test accuracy

Leave-one-out test: each recording is classified by a model trained on all the others. These are real numbers you can report.

Data

Camera recordings also save 3 photos each. The zip has one folder per sign (rest becomes none) and goes straight into ml/finetune_gesture_recognizer.ipynb to fine-tune Google's model on your signs.

Export from each phone or laptop and merge, so the model learns from everyone's hands. train.py reads the same file for the Python version.
